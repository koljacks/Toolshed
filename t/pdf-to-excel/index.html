About PDF to Excel Converter

Recover tables from a PDF into a spreadsheet by reading text positions. It runs entirely inside your browser — your files are never uploaded to a server, there is no signup, and there is no watermark on anything you produce.

How it works

  1. Open PDF to Excel Converter above.
  2. Add your file or enter your details — nothing leaves your device.
  3. Adjust the options, then download the result.
