Reporting a bug

The more specific you are, the faster it gets fixed. Useful details:

  • which tool, and what you were trying to do
  • what happened instead of what you expected
  • your browser and device

Please do not email the file itself. If a particular document breaks a tool, describe it — how many pages, whether it is a scan, roughly how large — and I can usually reproduce it.

Requesting a tool

Suggestions are welcome. Tools that can run in the browser are far easier to add than ones needing a server, so mention what you are trying to accomplish rather than only naming a format.

Corrections

If a calculator gives a figure you believe is wrong — particularly the GST, EMI or SIP tools — please tell me and show your working. Correctness matters more here than features.
